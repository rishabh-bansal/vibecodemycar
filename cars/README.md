# Cars: what people learned

One page per car: what's known about it from real cars, control unit by control unit. These pages are where
owners write down what worked, what didn't, and the quirks nobody documents.

| Car | Platform | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Škoda Kushaq | MQB-A0-IN | [skoda-kushaq.md](skoda-kushaq.md) |

Your car isn't here? Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) to `<brand>-<model>.md` and fill in what you know.
Even a few lines help. How to write a good finding: [docs/contributing/sharing-a-finding.md](../docs/contributing/sharing-a-finding.md).

**Never include a VIN, registration number or serial numbers.** Part numbers are fine.
